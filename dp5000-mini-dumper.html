Questions

About the DP5000

Will it really fit through a doorway?

715 mm at its narrowest, on a frame 800 mm across. A standard UK internal door opening is 838 mm and a back door is usually 813 mm, so yes — but measure the frame rather than the door, and watch for a threshold strip that eats the height.

How much is 500 kg in barrow loads?

About twelve. A builder's barrow holds roughly 40 kg of wet spoil before it becomes unmanageable. On a 20 metre path that is the difference between an afternoon and a morning.

Can it climb a slope with a full load?

It is built for it — the photographs on this page are of exactly that. Take slopes straight up or straight down and never across; a tracked machine tips sideways with very little warning once a loose load shifts.

Will it mark a lawn?

Rubber tracks spread the load far better than wheels, but low ground pressure reduces marking rather than removing it. On soft or newly laid ground, board out a route.

What maintenance does it need?

Engine oil and air filter to the engine maker's schedule, track tension checked regularly, and the hydraulic level checked before each session. Ask us for a price and delivery time on tracks and belts.
